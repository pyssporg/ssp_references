# IMP-005: Formalize Design Decisions Log from todo.md

**ID:** IMP-005
**Status:** proposed
**Theme:** Design Decision Traceability — Decisions Layer (KM-005)

**Evidence:**
- `todo.md` L40-49 has a "Current Decisions" section with 4 bullet points, each stating a decision but not the rationale behind it.
- `todo.md` is primarily a work-in-progress planning document, not a durable decisions archive.
- KM-005 requires a "Technical Decisions" layer that bridges architecture to build details — currently this layer is empty (IMP-003 addresses ADRs for architectural decisions; this candidate addresses smaller design decisions).
- No other location in the repo captures why specific implementation decisions were made (e.g., why signal_propagation fixtures use external SSV vs inline parameters, why three backends were chosen).

**Current pain or risk:**
Design decisions are trapped in bullet-point form without rationale, context, or alternatives considered. When a future task revisits a decision area, the original reasoning is invisible or must be reconstructed from git history. This directly contributes to "divergence within the implementation."

**Proposed improvement:**
Expand the `todo.md` "Current Decisions" section (or create a separate `DECISIONS.md`) to include:
1. Decision title.
2. Context — what prompted the decision.
3. Options considered.
4. Chosen approach.
5. Rationale — why this option was chosen.
6. Consequences — what constraints or tradeoffs this creates.
7. Date or commit reference.

**Expected benefit:**
- Captures lightweight design decisions that may not warrant a full ADR but still benefit from recorded rationale.
- Reduces re-litigation of settled design choices.
- Provides a searchable reference for agents and contributors.

**Risk and blast radius:**
- **Risk:** Low. Restructuring a section of `todo.md` or adding a new file. No code changes.
- **Risk of overlap with ADR mechanism:** IMP-003 covers architectural ADRs. IMP-005 covers smaller-scope design decisions. The boundary must be clear to avoid duplication.
- **Blast radius:** Single file (`todo.md` section expansion or creation of `DECISIONS.md`). No directory changes.

**Suggested priority:** Medium (lower than IMP-001/IMP-002, can depend on IMP-003's ADR template)

**Task contract seed:**
Expand the "Current Decisions" section in `todo.md` (or migrate to `DECISIONS.md`) to include rationale for each decision. For each decision, add: (1) Context — why the decision was needed, (2) The chosen approach, (3) Rationale — why this approach was picked over alternatives, (4) Consequences — what constraints or tradeoffs this creates. Extend the section with 2-3 additional decisions implicitly present in the codebase (e.g., "signal-propagation fixtures use deterministic algebraic FMU building blocks with predictable output", "comparison is engine-to-engine only for now"). Do not modify any code, other documentation, or FIXTURE.md files.

**Notes:** IMP-005 is deliberately lighter than IMP-003 (ADR mechanism). ADRs track architectural decisions; IMP-005 tracks smaller design decisions and implementation choices. If IMP-003 is implemented first, the ADR template could be reused here in a lighter form.
